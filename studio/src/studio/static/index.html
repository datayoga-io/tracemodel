When do we pay each line of each contract, and how much? Projects, engagements and payment lines are separate. An engagement points at its project and its contract, and a line points at its engagement. A pointer is an item like any other: it shows as a column, and the calculations follow it to find a line's project.

Model is the data: dimensions, and the items on them, entered or calculated. Each model table holds the items that share the same dimensions. A view is a saved layout over model items. It holds no data of its own, so editing a cell in a view edits the model.

Assumptions in this example
  • An engagement belongs to one project and comes from one contract. A contract that covers two projects has two engagements, one for each.
  • Each line has one date term and one price term.
  • Price bases: a fixed amount, a rate times the project's residential area, a rate times its retail area, or a percent of its sale value.
  • Milestones are shared by all projects. Each project has its own dates.
  • This page reads a real tracemodel model through an API. The formulas are the model's own, and a change is uncommitted until you commit it.
  • Pointers are required: you can change one to another valid label, but not clear it.
  • Every date item has date parts, like the pandas dt accessor: payment_date.dt.year, .dt.quarter, .dt.month, .dt.fiscal_year and .dt.fiscal_quarter. They lay out like dimensions. Month and quarter are of the year, so nest them under the year for a timeline. The fiscal year starts in April in this example.
  • A contract's committed amount is the sum of the amounts of the engagements that point at it, across projects. It should not exceed the contract value.
  • A blank cell means nothing was entered. n/a means someone marked it as not applicable, for example Hillside Court has no penthouses. Defined only keeps n/a and hides blanks.
